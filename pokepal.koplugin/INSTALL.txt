POKEPAL 0.2.0 - CHARMANDER EDITION

A quiet Pokemon companion for a jailbroken, touch-screen Kindle.
Starts with Charmander. Grow into Charmeleon and then Charizard.

INSTALL

1. Have a working KOReader installation on your Kindle.
   For firmware 5.17.1, use the kindlehf build. Your existing KUAL
   launcher can open KOReader if it already works with your setup.
   This plugin does not launch by itself from KUAL.

   Official KOReader download:
   https://github.com/koreader/koreader/releases

   Verified current package at creation (4 October 2026):
   koreader-kindlehf-v2026.07.1.zip
   https://github.com/koreader/koreader/releases/download/v2026.07.1/koreader-kindlehf-v2026.07.1.zip

   Official installation guide:
   https://github.com/koreader/koreader/wiki/Installation-on-Kindle-devices

2. Connect the Kindle to your computer by USB.
   Open the PokePal ZIP and copy its koreader folder into the top
   level of the Kindle drive, merging folders if asked.
   This only adds the pokepal.koplugin folder.

   The resulting path on the Kindle drive should be:
   koreader/plugins/pokepal.koplugin/main.lua

   Do not leave an extra PokePal-v0.2.0 folder around that path.

3. Safely eject and unplug the Kindle. Quit and reopen KOReader
   so it discovers the plugin.

4. In KOReader, tap the top of the screen to open its menu.
   Open Tools (wrench) > More tools > PokePal - Pokemon companion.

   If it is absent, look in Tools > More tools > Plugin management
   and enable PokePal, then restart KOReader. A customized menu
   may put it elsewhere; search the menu for PokePal.

5. Charmander is waiting in the meadow. A brief idle pose plays on
   opening; tap Charmander to see it again. Exit returns to KOReader.

PLAY

Feed: one berry restores 24 food and adds 2 bond. Save berries
when your partner is full. Start with 6 berries.

Play: remember a three-bush path, hide it, then repeat it.
Costs 10 energy, restores 20 joy, adds 4 bond and 5 XP.
A perfect path earns 8 XP. Ready again after 30 minutes.
You can leave an unfinished game without losing anything.

Train: choose a move to practice. Costs 18 energy and 8 food,
adds 12 XP and 2 bond. Ready again after one hour.
Practice moves unlock at levels 1, 7, 20 and 36.
Move unlocks and care rewards are this game's own rules.

Rest: toggle a nap. Energy recovers at 12 per hour while napping,
or 4 per hour while awake. Food declines more slowly during naps.
Wake your partner before playing, training or exploring.

Clean: tidy the nest when it gets dirty. Adds joy and 2 bond.

Basket: collect 5 berries once every 24 hours, measured from the
previous collection. The basket also works during an expedition.

Explore: send your partner on an offline expedition. The game does
not have to stay open. Reopen it and collect the result after:
  Meadow:    30 minutes; 12 energy; 3 berries and 12 XP
  Coast:      2 hours;   20 energy; 5 berries and 22 XP
  Moon Cave:  4 hours;   28 energy; 8 berries and 35 XP
Each expedition adds 4 bond and a Pokemon sighting to the journal.
Rewards are claimed once. Discoveries are collected, not playable
partners in this edition.

Profile: see your partner's progression. Each 20 XP adds a level.
  Charmander -> Charmeleon: level 16 (300 XP) and at least 30 bond
  Charmeleon -> Charizard: level 36 (700 XP) and at least 30 bond
Evolution is your choice: tap the enabled evolution button.
The levels follow the Charmander line; bond is an extra pet-game
requirement. Care meters, memories and XP survive evolution.

Journal: browse discovered Pokemon, recent activity and badges.
Activity: review the last 24 actions, with their dates and times.
More: animation settings, a screen refresh, help and profile.
The Play, Train and Basket buttons show their remaining cooldowns.

Your partner never dies. Needs decline gently and stop at forgiving
minimums after a long absence. Two or three brief visits a day are
enough for a relaxed game. There are no alerts or deadlines.

SPRITES AND BATTERY

Charmander, Charmeleon and Charizard have distinct idle, sleeping,
walking and attack animations sampled from real Pokemon Mystery
Dungeon sprite sheets. Eating poses appear where the source has
them. The earlier Black/White animated sprite frames remain for
the journal and other Pokemon. Source sheets are attributed in
CREDITS.txt and assets/pose-sources.json. All display frames are
converted to grayscale and enlarged with nearest-neighbour scaling.
The game is completely offline after installation.

Eco (default for new saves): 2 frames over about 1.6 seconds.
Gentle: the full short pose, up to 6 frames for the Charmander line
and up to 8 for other Pokemon, at about 2 frames per second.
Off: still sprites. Every burst settles to a still image. Existing
saves keep their previous animation preference.

Only the sprite/effect region refreshes during a burst. Normal
actions refresh the interface; a clearing refresh runs every
12 actions. You can also use More > Refresh screen.

There is no animation loop, background polling, Wi-Fi requirement,
sleep inhibition or wake alarm. An opening idle/sleep pose runs once
and stops; tapping the sprite or taking an action starts another
short burst. Power-button sleep cancels it, and resuming shows a
still frame without restarting it. Care, rests and expeditions are
calculated from elapsed time when you next interact. The sky
changes between day and night on interaction, using the Kindle's
clock. KOReader continues to handle power-button sleep.

For the lowest drain, choose Eco or Off, use airplane mode if you
normally do, and exit or put the Kindle to sleep between visits.
Actual battery drain and animation quality have not been measured
on a physical Kindle. E-ink response and ghosting vary by model.

SAVES AND REMOVAL

The save is normally koreader/settings/pokepal.dat on the Kindle
USB drive. The previous snapshot is pokepal.dat.old. The exact
location follows KOReader's settings directory if customized.

Progress saves after actions, when starting, on sleep and on exit.
Writes use a temporary file and atomic rename, with the previous
valid snapshot retained. An unreadable current save falls back to
the previous one; two unreadable copies are left untouched.

Back up both save files before changing devices or reinstalling.
To remove the game, quit KOReader and remove only
koreader/plugins/pokepal.koplugin. Saved progress is kept.

TROUBLESHOOTING

KOReader does not launch: first make its normal launcher work.
Use kindlehf for firmware 5.17.1, and consult the official guide.
This game cannot fix a broken KUAL/KOReader installation.

Game absent: check the exact folder path, enable external plugins
if you have disabled them, and restart KOReader.

Game crashes or pictures are missing: recopy the entire plugin
folder, including assets. KOReader's crash.log may show the cause.

Ghosting or animation is distracting: use Eco or Off and tap
More > Refresh screen.

Save failed: check free storage and copy the existing save files
to your computer before attempting repairs.

Save unreadable: back up pokepal.dat and pokepal.dat.old first.
Do not erase them unless you want a new adventure. A manual fresh
start can be made by renaming both files while KOReader is closed.

DEVICE STATUS

This is an installable preview release. The Lua rules, save recovery,
touch dispatch and animation lifecycle were tested on a computer.
Layouts were rendered at Basic, Paperwhite and Oasis-style screen
sizes. That does not certify firmware/device compatibility.
Please begin with Eco mode on your Kindle and confirm feed, exit,
power-button sleep, resume and save persistence.

Artwork credits and validation details are included alongside
this file in the plugin folder.
