POKEPAL 0.4.0

KUAL EDITION (STANDALONE PREVIEW)

You need a jailbroken touchscreen Kindle and KUAL. KOReader is not needed.
Copy the ZIP's extensions folder to the top level of the Kindle drive.
The executable should be at extensions/pokepal/pokepal.
Safely eject, then open KUAL > PokePal > Play PokePal.

This ARMv7 build initially targets the 2021 Paperwhite in portrait mode.
It has not been tested on a physical Kindle. Older button-only Kindles
are not supported. A bundled C/Lua runtime avoids relying on the
firmware's floating-point libc ABI; it does not prove device compatibility.

Exit saves and returns to Kindle Home. Going to sleep saves and closes
PokePal. Open it from KUAL to resume.
Saves: extensions/pokepal/data/pokepal.dat and pokepal.dat.old.
Errors: extensions/pokepal/data/launch.log.
Keep data when updating. To remove the app, exit first, back up data,
then remove extensions/pokepal by USB.

KOREADER EDITION

Install KOReader separately. Copy the ZIP's koreader folder to the top
level of the Kindle drive. Merge folders; don't replace your installation.
The plugin belongs at koreader/plugins/pokepal.koplugin/main.lua.
Restart KOReader, then open Tools > More tools > PokePal.
If missing, check Plugin management and restart again.
Official downloads: https://github.com/koreader/koreader/releases

Older saves still load. Back up pokepal.dat and pokepal.dat.old in
KOReader's settings folder before updating. To move progress to KUAL,
close both editions and copy those files into extensions/pokepal/data/.
They remain separate saves after that.

PLAYING

Choose one of eight starters. Tap your partner for a short animation.
Feed spends one berry and restores 24 food. Basket gives five berries
every 24 hours. Play is a three-step memory game, ready every 30 minutes.
A perfect path gives 8 XP; a missed step gives 5 XP. Training gives 12 XP
once an hour and costs 18 energy and 8 food. Moves and unlocks are
PokePal rules, not main-series battle learnsets.

Rest restores energy faster. Wake your partner before active play.
Clean tidies the nest when it is dirty.

Explore keeps track of time with the game closed:
  Meadow: 30 minutes, 12 energy, 3 berries, 12 XP.
  Coast: 2 hours, 20 energy, 5 berries, 22 XP.
  Moon Cave: 4 hours, 28 energy, 8 berries, 35 XP.
Collect the result afterward. Each trail chooses unseen Pokemon while
it has any left. All 151 Kanto Pokemon and nine Johto starter forms
can be found across the three trails.

Tap a Pokemon in Journal to view types and poses, or choose it as your
partner. Each species keeps its XP and bond. Food, energy, berries, dirt
and cooldowns are shared; switching does not refill them. You cannot
switch during a trip or a nap.

Profile shows evolution requirements: the listed XP and at least
30 bond. Eevee has three Kanto choices. No trades or purchased stones
are needed. Earlier forms remain available in the journal.

ANIMATION

All 160 species have Idle, Sleep, Walk and Attack. 33 also have Eat.
Unavailable poses are disabled in the journal. Feeding uses Idle if Eat
is absent. Persian and Arcanine have still Idle poses in the source.
Eco uses two frames; Gentle uses up to four; Off disables animation.
Only front-facing frames are included. Nothing animates between actions.
Use More > Refresh screen if ghosting builds up.

SAVES AND TROUBLESHOOTING

The previous valid save is kept as pokepal.dat.old. If the main save is
damaged, PokePal tries that copy. If both are unreadable, it stops and
leaves them in place. Back them up before trying a repair.

If KUAL cannot start the app, confirm the whole extension was copied,
then read data/launch.log. For touch problems, include the exact model
and firmware from Settings > Device Options > Device Info in your report.
Physical touch, sleep and battery checks are still pending.

Your partner does not die while you are away. No Wi-Fi is needed to play.
