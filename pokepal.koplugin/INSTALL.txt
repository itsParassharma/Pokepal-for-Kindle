POKEPAL 1.0.9

KUAL
Needs a jailbroken touchscreen Kindle with KUAL installed.

1. Unzip PokePal-v1.0.9-kual.zip.
2. Copy extensions onto your Kindle drive, next to documents.
   Merge folders if asked.
3. Eject, then open KUAL > PokePal > Play PokePal.

KOReader is not required for this version.

KOREADER
Needs KOReader installed on your Kindle.

1. Unzip PokePal-v1.0.9-koreader.zip.
2. Copy koreader onto your Kindle drive. Merge with the existing folder.
3. Restart KOReader. Choose Open PokePal in the welcome, or Tools > PokePal.

The welcome appears once. Shortcut help explains how to assign Open PokePal
to a gesture or QuickMenu. Later closes the welcome without starting a game.
Hold the PokePal entry in Tools to see the help again. Existing gestures
are left as they are. Keep the folder named pokepal.koplugin; KOReader uses
the .koplugin ending to find plugins.

SAVES
Both editions use pokepal/pokepal.dat on the Kindle drive.
Back up the whole pokepal folder before updating. Keep it when replacing
the app files. Update both editions if you use both.

Older saves are copied across on first launch; the originals are kept.
If both editions had separate saves, the most recently used one is chosen.
The old locations are extensions/pokepal/data and KOReader's settings folder.
Close the other copy before opening PokePal in a different reader.

READING REWARDS
Open PokePal once, then read. Return to collect 1 berry and 3 XP for every
15 recorded minutes. Leftover minutes carry over. Old reading history
isn't rewarded when you first turn it on.
Kindle's reader needs supported timing files beside the book.
KOReader needs this plugin enabled; Reading statistics can stay off.
Sleep and time spent in PokePal do not count. KOReader counts at most
two minutes per page and skips turns faster than five seconds.
More > Reading rewards shows progress and lets you turn rewards off.

OPTIONAL SETTINGS
More > Preferences has larger text, stronger contrast and automatic rest
when the Kindle sleeps. KOReader can also show your sleeping partner on
its sleep screen or add a small reading footer. These settings start off.
KUAL keeps the Kindle's normal sleep screen. No screensaver add-on is needed.

AROUND THE NEST
Explore > Trips > As you read starts a trail that advances with recorded reading.
Timed trips are the default and don't need reading rewards.
More > Reading rewards > Your egg has eggs for 3 berries. Read for 120 minutes, then tap
Hatch your egg. It joins the journal and keeps your current partner.
The same minutes advance your egg, trail and regular reading rewards.
Home keeps your pet, care stats, bond and XP together.
Feed, play, train, rest, clean and collect daily berries from home.
More holds reading rewards, backgrounds, decorations and backups.
Decorations cost berries once and can be placed or hidden in any background.
Profile > Nickname lets you name your partner. The name stays when it evolves.
Profile > Change companion lets you switch partners. Choose from all 386
Pokemon opens the full list, even for Pokemon you haven't met. Tap one and
confirm. Each keeps its XP, bond and nickname. The full list is also
available when starting a new game, alongside the 11 quick starters.
Use the Kanto, Johto and Hoenn tabs to browse by region. Every Pokemon has
ten animation previews. Training moves use different poses as they unlock.
More > Animation lets you choose Gentle, Eco or Off.
More > Reading rewards > Weekly goal offers 60, 120 or 180 minutes. Reach it for 5 berries
and 10 XP. Weeks start on Monday and count minutes collected that week.
Last visit in the reading menu shows your rewards, trail and egg progress.
More > Backups saves your game and reading records together. Tap Preview
before restoring. Your current game is kept under Before last restore.
If a restore stops midway, close and reopen PokePal to finish it.
Copy the pokepal folder to your computer to keep a backup off the Kindle.
Use matching builds in both readers. Back up the whole pokepal folder
before updating; older builds cannot read the new save fields.

HELP
Missing from KUAL? Check extensions/pokepal/menu.json is on your Kindle.
Missing from KOReader? Check koreader/plugins/pokepal.koplugin/main.lua,
then enable PokePal in Plugin management and restart.
KUAL startup errors are logged in extensions/pokepal/data/launch.log.
Device notes: native/README.md in the source download.

Tap a journal entry to switch partners or view animations.
More > Refresh screen clears a ghosted screen.
