PokePal is an unofficial personal fan game.

Pokemon names, characters and original sprite artwork belong to
their respective rights holders, including Nintendo, Game Freak
and Creatures. These assets are not covered by the code license.

Animated Gen V sprites are sourced from the PokeAPI sprites
repository. Exact source URLs and sampled source frame indices
are recorded in assets/sources.json.
https://github.com/PokeAPI/sprites

Charmander, Charmeleon and Charizard pose animations use front-facing
frames from PMDCollab SpriteCollab sprite sheets. Idle, Sleep, Walk,
Attack and available Eat sheets were cropped, resized and converted
to four-level grayscale. Exact sheets and sampled indices are in
assets/pose-sources.json.
https://github.com/PMDCollab/SpriteCollab

The source sheets credit CHUNSOFT for the original frames. The
Charmeleon Eat sheet credits bwappi and Grimlin; Charizard Sleep
credits Emboarger. Artist and license records are supplied in
SPRITE-SOURCE-CREDITS.txt. The PMDCollab source includes CC BY-NC 4.0
and PMDCollab artist terms; copies are included as SPRITE-LICENSE*.md.
The sprites and Pokemon characters are not covered by the game's
Lua code license.

Charmander-line evolution levels were checked against PokeAPI:
https://pokeapi.co/api/v2/evolution-chain/2/

KOReader provides the device input, image rendering, screen refresh
and suspend/resume facilities. It is installed separately.
https://github.com/koreader/koreader

The game's Lua implementation, native pixel icons and UI layout
were created for this request. The meadow layout was inspired by
the user's reference image. Grayscale conversion and brief sampled
animations are adaptations for e-ink displays.
