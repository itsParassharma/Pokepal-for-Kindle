PokePal is an unofficial Pokemon fan game.
Pokemon names, characters and original artwork belong to their respective
owners, including Nintendo, Game Freak, Creatures and CHUNSOFT.
They are not covered by the code license.

SPRITES
https://github.com/PMDCollab/SpriteCollab
Revision: 4702f10d888d19e804c5986a91170dfc92ae01b8
The package includes all 386 Pokemon from Kanto, Johto and Hoenn.
Idle, Sleep, Walk, Attack, Eat, Hop, Rotate, Charge, Swing and Double sheets
were cropped, scaled with nearest-neighbour sampling and converted to
four-level grayscale. The five new poses keep the body close to its idle
size, with movement bounded inside the sprite tile.
Missing eating poses use PokePal's berry and chewing motion, derived from
the credited idle sprites. Still poses use added breathing motion. These
additions are recorded in the source manifest. Every species has ten
moving poses, with at most four frames per animation.
All 386 species also include down, left, up and right walking frames from
the cardinal rows of those same sheets. assets/walk-sources.json records
the rows, sampled frames, source hashes and original per-species credits.

assets/pose-sources.json records sheet URLs, SHA-256, metadata, selected
frames, motion and per-species credit files. assets/credits/ contains the
original records. Artist names are in SPRITE-PMDCollab-credit_names.txt.
The upstream license is in SPRITE-PMDCollab-LICENSE.md; original license
files are also retained. SpriteCollab requires attribution and non-commercial
use. Its terms do not transfer ownership of Pokemon or original game art.

SPECIES DATA
https://github.com/PokeAPI/pokeapi
Revision: 2bf639e5572812a67d2b94903fdcb9d095f5aeb2
Names, types and family relationships come from its CSVs.
The battle type chart comes from type_efficacy.csv at the same revision.
XP thresholds, moves, rewards and cooldowns are PokePal rules.
Legacy starter thresholds are kept for save compatibility.

CODE AND RUNTIME
Shared game, original pixel icons and UI: MIT (LICENSE-CODE.txt).
Classic map tiles, trainers and battle sprites are original Pokemon game art.
They are not covered by the code's MIT license. Pokemon game artwork belongs
to Nintendo, Game Freak and Creatures. Sources and conversion records:
https://github.com/pret/pokecrystal
Revision: 5c877ef55197039c9676e8e3eec6d254b1c40fe8
Crystal supplies map tiles, trainers and front/back sprites for Pokemon 1-251.
https://github.com/pret/pokeemerald
Revision: 731ad5bfd6e6f265508d0efcca0ba42f9dcf5881
Emerald supplies front/back sprites for Pokemon 252-386, reduced to four shades.
assets/classic/sources.json records the source paths and SHA-256 hashes.
tools/import_classic.py builds the maps and PNG packs used by both Lua hosts.
The opening story also adapts Crystal's map layouts, collisions, doors,
characters and event sequence at that same pinned revision. Dialogue is
condensed for PokePal. assets/story/sources.json records the source paths
and hashes; tools/import_story.py rebuilds the nine maps and their art.
Original game maps, artwork and story are not covered by the code's MIT license.
Field Pokemon keep the credited SpriteCollab walking directions, resized to
24-pixel canvases and the same four shades. Their upstream terms still apply.

The KOReader edition uses the separately installed KOReader runtime.
The standalone host is GPL-3.0-or-later and uses FBInk, Lua, stb and
Bitstream Vera. Runtime licenses and corresponding source are included
with the standalone release. KOReader is not required for that edition.

FBInk: https://github.com/NiLuJe/FBInk
Lua: https://www.lua.org/

https://github.com/bomberfish/kdoom
No DOOM data or kdoom source is included.
