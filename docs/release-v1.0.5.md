Read a few chapters, then check in on your Pokémon. PokePal now turns reading time into berries and XP.

- Every 15 recorded minutes earns 1 berry and 3 XP. Leftover minutes carry over.
- Automatic tracking for KOReader and supported Kindle reading records.
- KUAL and KOReader share your save. Existing progress is copied across and the originals are kept.
- Check progress or turn rewards off under **More > Reading rewards**.

### KUAL

1. Unzip **PokePal-v1.0.5-kual.zip**.
2. Copy `extensions` onto your Kindle drive, next to `documents`.
3. Eject, then open **KUAL > PokePal > Play PokePal**.

### KOReader

1. Unzip **PokePal-v1.0.5-koreader.zip**.
2. Merge its `koreader` folder with the one on your Kindle.
3. Restart KOReader, then open **Tools > More tools > PokePal**.

Open PokePal once before reading to start counting. Kindle tracking depends on the book's saved timing data. KOReader needs the PokePal plugin enabled.

KUAL needs a jailbroken touchscreen Kindle. If you use both editions, update both. Back up the `pokepal` folder on your Kindle drive before future updates. [Tracking details](https://github.com/itsParassharma/Pokepal-for-Kindle/blob/main/docs/reading-rewards.md).
