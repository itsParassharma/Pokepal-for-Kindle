- Play the Crystal story through Goldenrod City: catch Pokémon, build a party of six and take on Falkner, Bugsy and Whitney. Open **Explore → Story** to start.
- Add your partner to SimpleUI with live stats, short animations and optional Tiny Critter images.
- Simpler battles, without guarding or charging, and fixes for reading scans on non-Kindle KOReader devices.

Back up your whole `pokepal` folder before updating. Story saves use version 13, which older builds cannot read. PokePal keeps a **Before story upgrade** backup in **More → Backups**. Restore it in this build before downgrading, and update both editions if you use KOReader and KUAL.

The story ends after Whitney’s Plain Badge. Physical Kindle touch, e-ink refresh and power-button/cover sleep and wake still need testing.
