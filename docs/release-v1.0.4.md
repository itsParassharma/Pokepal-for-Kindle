PokePal now runs straight from KUAL, with no KOReader needed. The KOReader version is still included.

- All 160 Pokémon have idle, eating, sleeping, walking and attack animations.
- Switch partners without losing their XP or bond.
- Explore to find Pokémon you haven't met yet.
- Eco mode now plays both frames of short animations.

### KUAL

1. Unzip **PokePal-v1.0.4-kual.zip**.
2. Copy `extensions` onto your Kindle drive, next to `documents`.
3. Eject, then open **KUAL > PokePal > Play PokePal**.

### KOReader

1. Unzip **PokePal-v1.0.4-koreader.zip**.
2. Merge its `koreader` folder with the one on your Kindle.
3. Restart KOReader. Open **Tools > More tools > PokePal**.

KUAL needs a jailbroken touchscreen Kindle. Back up your save before updating.
