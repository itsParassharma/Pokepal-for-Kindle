- Explore Fern Town and four wild areas, recruit Pokémon with berries, build a party of up to six and store your collection in the PC box. Challenge Gym leaders, fresh trainer teams and the Battle Tower.
- New Crystal maps and trainer artwork, front and back battle sprites, and walking animations in all four directions for all 386 Pokémon.
- Major bug fixes include overlapping town messages, messages lingering on other screens, resting partners getting stuck after battles, and missing home backgrounds after waking.

With [Bookshelf](https://github.com/AndyHazz/bookshelf.koplugin) 6.0 or later, your partner can sit on the KOReader home screen. Add **PokePal** in the layout editor and tap it to play. Thanks to [AndyHazz](https://github.com/AndyHazz) for the module.

Back up your `pokepal` folder before updating. Older versions cannot read saves upgraded by Explore. Update both editions if you use both.
