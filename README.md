# PokePal for Kindle

A Pokémon pet for your Kindle. Check in between chapters, feed your partner or send it exploring.

![PokePal](docs/kindle-preview.png)

Choose from 160 Pokémon, each with idle, eating, sleeping, walking and attack animations. Your partners keep their XP and bond when you switch.

[Download 1.0.4](https://github.com/itsParassharma/Pokepal-for-Kindle/releases/tag/v1.0.4) · [Watch the trailer](https://github.com/itsParassharma/Pokepal-for-Kindle/releases/download/v1.0.4/PokePal-trailer.mp4)

## Install with KUAL

You need a jailbroken touchscreen Kindle with KUAL installed. KOReader is not required.

1. Download and unzip **PokePal-v1.0.4-kual.zip**.
2. Connect your Kindle by USB. Copy the `extensions` folder onto the Kindle drive, next to `documents`. Merge folders if asked.
3. Eject your Kindle, then open **KUAL > PokePal > Play PokePal**.

## Install with KOReader

You need KOReader installed on your Kindle.

1. Download and unzip **PokePal-v1.0.4-koreader.zip**.
2. Copy the `koreader` folder onto the Kindle drive. Merge it with the existing folder.
3. Restart KOReader, then open **Tools > More tools > PokePal**.

## Playing and saves

- Expeditions keep time while the game is closed. Collect your discoveries when you return.
- Tap a journal entry to choose a partner or try its animations.
- Choose **Eco**, **Gentle** or **Off** under **More**.
- Keep `extensions/pokepal/data` when updating KUAL. Back up your save first.
